# Sirea — Business Model Canvas

2026-09-21 · @Someone

One-page snapshot of the model discussed across the PRD and earlier brainstorming — useful for stress-testing the subscription + credit + revenue-share structure before it's locked in. Built on the PRD's English/international-first assumption.

| Block | Content |
| --- | --- |
| **Key Partners** | Payment processors (Stripe / Apple & Google in-app billing, per the English/international scope); video/voice infrastructure provider (if not fully self-built); identity/background-check data sources for Verified Pro review; recruited well-known astrologers who agree to a platform-granted badge as an early credibility/growth partnership. |
| **Key Activities** | Astrologer onboarding and badge review; content moderation and fraud-signal review; live video/voice session infrastructure maintenance; payment and wallet ledger management; discovery/feed curation and ranking. |
| **Key Resources** | The astrologer roster itself (supply-side liquidity is the core asset); the in-app video/voice platform; the badge and rating trust system; brand/name (Sirea) and its associated design system; founder's existing network (useful for early astrologer recruitment, per the recruited-badge strategy). |
| **Value Propositions** | *For users*: a trusted way to discover and book real astrologers (not just algorithmic horoscopes), plus a culturally distinct kahve falı offering, all without leaving the app. *For astrologers*: a distribution and monetization channel combining content, live sessions, and a flexible payout model (bundled or à la carte), open to both established professionals and newcomers. |
| **Customer Relationships** | Self-service for most interactions (booking, content browsing); personal/high-touch for live sessions themselves (the astrologer-user relationship is the product); community-style following/engagement via the content feed; platform-mediated trust via badges and ratings rather than the platform vouching for every astrologer individually. |
| **Channels** | The app itself (iOS, Android, web); astrologer-driven organic growth (their existing social following, the referral/invite mechanic); platform-side recruitment of known-name astrologers as a launch strategy; word-of-mouth via the kahve falı social/ritual use case. |
| **Customer Segments** | End users seeking astrological guidance or kahve falı (ranging from casual/curious to repeat paying clients); professional astrologers seeking a distribution and monetization channel; amateur/emerging astrologers seeking a low-barrier way to build an audience and reputation. |
| **Cost Structure** | Payment processing fees; video/voice infrastructure hosting and bandwidth; engineering (in-house, per current team plan); customer support and trust & safety review staffing; astrologer payouts (the largest variable cost, scaling with GMV); marketing/growth spend if any beyond organic and referral channels. |
| **Revenue Streams** | Subscription tiers (Free / Premium / Premium+Credit); revenue share on à la carte credit-based session bookings; revenue share on bundled/subscription-pooled sessions; credit wallet float (funds held between top-up and spend, though this is not a revenue stream itself, it affects cash flow timing). |
